Set North with your phone:
1. Open the compass app on your phone.
2. Hold the phone flat on top of the rover, both facing the same direction.
3. Rotate the rover slowly until the phone compass reads exactly 0° (N).
4. Keep the rover still and press Set North below.

Calibration and north offset are saved on the Pi and auto-load on every boot.
